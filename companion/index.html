🛡️ Military & Bank-Grade Architecture

Your Medical Privacy Shielded by Zero-Knowledge Encryption

At FES HealthTech, we firmly believe your biometric and nutritional data belong solely to you and your healthcare provider. We engineered Murshid Companion with state-of-the-art cryptography:

  • ✓ Pairwise Curve25519 (X25519) Cryptography: Generates distinct 32-byte key pairs inside your device's hardware-backed Keystore or Keychain.
  • ✓ Zero-Knowledge Cloud Infrastructure: Neither FES HealthTech nor our cloud servers can decrypt your meal entries, weights, or clinical forms.
  • ✓ Encrypted Google Drive Backups: Store your clinical history in your own personal Google Drive app vault, restorable instantly across devices.

Clear & Immediate Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Murshid Companion, data ownership, and daily tracking.

Is Murshid Companion completely free to use? +

Yes. Murshid Companion is 100% free for subscribers and clinic patients. There are no subscriptions, ads, or locked features. The app is provided to enhance your care under your supervising dietitian.

How do I receive my nutrition plan in the app? +

Once you enter your clinic connection code, your dietitian publishes your customized nutrition plan directly to your phone. It populates immediately under the "My Plan" tab with meal items, calories, macros, and exchange choices.

Does the app work without an internet connection? +

Yes. The app is built with an offline-first architecture (encrypted on-device local database). You can inspect your diet, log water, and track meal adherence entirely offline. When your connection returns, the encrypted logs sync automatically with your clinic.

How can I swap a meal item I don't have available? +

Simply open the meal card and tap "Swap Food". The app displays pre-approved ADA food group equivalents that match your target calories and macronutrients, letting you select an alternative on the fly.

What happens if I delete my account? +

You can delete your account at any time via Settings > Account Options > Delete Account (or through our web data deletion page). Deleting your account wipes your companion profile and unlinks all clinics. Please note that historical clinical records previously kept by your healthcare clinic remain under independent clinic custody, and you should contact your clinic directly to request medical record modifications.
