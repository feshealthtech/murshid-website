4 Dual Wallet System

The app operates on a dual wallet system consisting of two types of points, each with different usage rules and conditions:

🔵 Blue Points

  • Completely free and earned by watching ads and daily in-app rewards
  • Designated for local operations only, such as adding patients, performing metabolic calculations, and exporting PDF reports
  • Non-transferable to other accounts or refundable for cash in any form
  • May automatically expire 90 days after discontinued use
  • Are equivalent in value to local operation units and are not a currency with monetary value

🟡 Gold Points

  • Designated for smart data extraction (OCR) operations and cloud services requiring external cloud resources.
  • Can be acquired through a subscription plan (PRO), purchased as direct one-time packages via app stores, or earned through promotional rewards and referrals.
  • Subscription Quota Points: Users subscribed to the "Murshid Pro" plan receive a monthly quota of 500 Gold Points. These points are valid only during the active subscription billing cycle. They do not accumulate or roll over to the next month; any unused subscription points are automatically reset and replaced with the new cycle's quota of 500 points.
  • Expiration & Cancellation: If the subscription expires, is canceled, or fails to renew, any remaining Gold Points granted under the subscription quota will be immediately revoked and forfeited.
  • Permanent Points: Points earned through promotional actions, direct one-time package purchases, or ads remain permanent and valid for one full year from the acquisition date, and are not affected or revoked by subscription cancellation.
  • Consumed after successful execution of an operation and are non-refundable after consumption under any circumstances.
  • Managed in the cloud via Firebase to ensure integrity, prevent manipulation, and ensure cross-device synchronization.
  • Are not legal tender or financial instruments and cannot be converted into cash.
Important Note FES HealthTech reserves the right to modify point values and consumption rates with prior notice. Accumulated balances will not be affected by future modifications under the principle of acquired rights.
