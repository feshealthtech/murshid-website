Practical Clinical Scenario

Designing a Complete Custom Diet in 4 Minutes

How a clinician designs a tailored fat-loss and lean-mass preservation plan for an athletic patient:

Step 1

Select BMR Formula & Physical Activity Multiplier

The dietitian selects Katch-McArdle using the patient's recorded DEXA body fat percentage. Murshid computes BMR instantly, applies an activity factor of 1.55 (TDEE: 2,400 kcal), and incorporates a targeted 400 kcal deficit.

Step 2

Configure Macro Strategy

The clinician sets protein to 2.0g/kg body weight, carbohydrates to 40%, and the remainder to healthy fats. Meal targets across breakfast, lunch, workout snacks, and dinner are generated automatically.

Step 3

Select Foods from the Offline Bank

The practitioner searches items from the 13,000-food database. Dynamic balance meters turn green as macro and calorie goals are met with exact precision.

Step 4

PDF Export & Companion App Sync

The plan is exported into a high-resolution branded PDF and synchronized directly with the patient's Murshid Client companion app for seamless exchange-point meal swaps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers on mathematical models, point systems, and practice autonomy:

❓ Which predictive energy expenditure formulas are built into Murshid?

Murshid embeds the six internationally recognized clinical formulas: Mifflin-St Jeor (clinical gold standard), Harris-Benedict (Original & Revised), Katch-McArdle (lean body mass based), Cunningham (for elite athletes), Schofield (pediatric/adolescent), and DRI.

❓ What is the core philosophy of Murshid's Dual-Points System?

It bridges rigorous scientific precision for the clinician with practical adherence for the patient. While the dietitian balances exact calories, macros, and micronutrients, the patient interacts with intuitive ADA Exchange portion points allowing flexible meal swaps without sabotaging targets.

❓ Does the meal planning calculation engine work offline?

Yes. All predictive formulas, macro distributions, and the 13,000-item food database operate natively within the local Isar database, ensuring lightning-fast execution without requiring an internet connection.

❓ How does Murshid assist with critical micronutrient tracking?

As meals are compiled, dynamic live progress meters calculate cumulative sodium, potassium, phosphorus, and dietary fiber, allowing precise management of renal and cardiovascular dietary prescriptions.

❓ Can diet plans be exported into branded clinical PDFs?

Yes, with one click, dietitians can generate high-resolution PDF meal plans personalized with clinic logos, custom dietary notes, and organized meal timelines.

❓ Does the engine generate autonomous medical diagnoses or forced diets?

No. The system is strictly a computational, administrative, and design tool. Murshid is not a medical device (SaMD); all clinical and dietary prescriptions remain solely under the professional discretion of the licensed dietitian.
