The Documentation Challenge

Why Fragmented Clinical Patient Records Sabotage Outcomes

Practitioners face fragmented workflows that undermine continuity of care and client motivation:

📑

Scattered Clinical and Lab Records

InBody printouts in folders, blood test photos in chat threads, and dietary histories in physical notebooks prevent clinicians from seeing the full trajectory of a patient's health markers across time.

📉

Difficulty Demonstrating Visual Body Progress

Patients often obsess over total scale weight and get demotivated during plateaus. Lacking interactive visual charts that prove simultaneous fat loss and muscle gain leads to preventable drop-outs.

👶

Complex Pediatric Z-Score Plotting

Manually plotting height-for-age and BMI-for-age on paper WHO growth charts is tedious and slow during busy clinic schedules, creating risk of missing subtle growth faltering or childhood obesity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Answers to common questions regarding data security, OCR extraction, and pediatric charts:

❓ What clinical and biometric records are tracked within Patient 360?

Patient 360 aggregates demographic data, full medical and pharmaceutical histories, dietary allergies and preferences, longitudinal body composition data (InBody/DEXA metrics), digitized laboratory blood tests, and progressive visit graphs.

❓ How does the AI optical lab test extractor (OCR) work?

Clinicians photograph printed laboratory reports from any diagnostic facility. The OCR engine parses clinical biomarkers (fasting blood glucose, HbA1c, lipid fractions, kidney/liver panels, vitamins), structuring the values with standard reference ranges and color alerts.

❓ Does Murshid support official World Health Organization (WHO) pediatric growth standards?

Yes, Murshid incorporates the official WHO Growth Standards Z-Scores engine for children and adolescents (birth to 19 years), computing height-for-age, weight-for-age, and BMI-for-age to detect stunting or pediatric obesity.

❓ Where is patient data stored, and is it secure?

All patient files, measurements, and clinical records are stored exclusively on the practitioner's local device within an embedded, encrypted Isar database. No patient data is sent to external cloud servers, ensuring strict HIPAA/GDPR compliance.

❓ Can patients complete pre-consultation questionnaires prior to the visit?

Yes, Murshid supports end-to-end encrypted (TweetNaCl) digital intake questionnaires, allowing patients to complete medical history and dietary habits on their own phones before entering the consultation.

❓ Does the CRM system generate autonomous medical diagnoses?

No. The system functions as an administrative, documentation, and visual tracking tool. Murshid is not a medical device (SaMD); all clinical and diagnostic evaluations remain solely under the professional discretion of the licensed dietitian.
