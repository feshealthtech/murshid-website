Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clarifications regarding hosting, custom subdomains, and practice autonomy:

❓ Does building a clinic website in Murshid require web coding skills or separate hosting?

No coding, domain registration, or server hosting is required. Murshid deploys a mobile-optimized public clinic landing page in seconds, hosted automatically on secure high-speed cloud infrastructure with complimentary SSL encryption.

❓ What sections and details are showcased on the generated clinic page?

The page showcases the dietitian's biography, credentials, and practice licenses; consultation menu and subscription packages with transparent pricing; clinic operational hours; interactive Google Maps location pins; and direct WhatsApp/Phone booking buttons.

❓ How does the print-ready marketing poster generator work?

The system automatically produces high-resolution (300 DPI) PDF vector designs formatted for standard frames (A4, A3) and vertical roll-up banners, complete with the clinic's logo and a scannable QR code directing clients to the clinic portal.

❓ Can prospective patients book appointments directly through the website?

Yes. Interactive booking buttons launch direct WhatsApp conversations pre-filled with the selected consultation tier, and enable clients to fill out end-to-end encrypted pre-visit intake questionnaires.

❓ Can package fees and consultation pricing be updated anytime?

Practitioners can edit fees, add new service packages, or adjust clinic hours anytime from inside the Murshid app. Changes reflect live on their public website in real time.

❓ Does the clinic website generate automated medical diagnoses for visitors?

No. The website serves strictly as an informational and administrative marketing portal. Murshid is not a medical device (SaMD) and provides no autonomous clinical diagnoses or remote prescriptions over the web.
