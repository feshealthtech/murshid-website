Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Clinic Billing & ERP

Everything you need to know about financial management, tax compliance, and patient ledgers in Murshid.

❓ Does Murshid support ascending, non-repeatable sequential invoice numbering?

Yes. Murshid features an internal Serial Counter Engine that automatically issues strictly sequential invoice and official receipt numbers tailored to your practice, ensuring full accounting integrity.

❓ How does Murshid track installment debts and remaining subscription balances?

When a client subscribes to a multi-visit nutrition program and pays a partial down-payment, Murshid instantly logs the outstanding remainder as an active debt in their Patient 360 ledger, reminding the dietitian at each subsequent appointment.

❓ Can I print and export branded patient financial statements as PDFs?

Yes. With a single click, practitioners can export a professional PDF financial statement featuring the clinic logo, an itemized list of consultation fees, recorded payment dates, and current remaining balance for reimbursement or personal records.

❓ How does the platform compute true practice net profitability?

Murshid includes an operational expense tracker (clinic rent, team salaries, InBody device servicing, marketing overhead, utility bills). By comparing total collected consultation revenue against recorded expenditures, it provides real-time net profit margins.

❓ Do exported invoices support regulatory e-invoicing standards (like ZATCA QR codes)?

Yes. Generated invoices and receipt vouchers feature standardized QR codes and metadata formatted according to official tax and billing authorities in the GCC and wider region.

❓ Does the clinic billing and financial engine function without internet access?

Yes. The entire billing engine, ledger database, and expense calculator are embedded locally on the practitioner's device using encrypted local storage (Isar), operating 100% offline without cloud latency or subscription lockouts.
