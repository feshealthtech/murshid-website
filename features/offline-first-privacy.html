Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Offline-First Architecture

Everything you need to know about security, on-device data storage, and portability in Murshid.

❓ Does Murshid function entirely without an internet connection?

Yes. Murshid was engineered under a strict Offline-First architectural paradigm. The comprehensive 13,000-food bank, 10,000-drug reference atlas, energy calculation engines, and patient records run 100% locally from your device memory without requiring network access.

❓ Where are patient clinical and financial records stored?

All clinical records, biometric assessments, and accounting ledgers reside strictly on the dietitian's local device inside a high-speed, encrypted Isar database. Patient data is never uploaded, shared, sold, or used to train external artificial intelligence models.

❓ How do practitioners back up or migrate clinic data to a new device?

Murshid provides a one-click Encrypted Local Backup feature. Practitioners can export an encrypted database archive directly onto a secure USB flash drive or physical storage medium and restore it on any new device in seconds without third-party cloud intermediaries.

❓ What happens to sensitive clinic data upon logout on shared devices?

Murshid implements a Leak-Proof Session Purge protocol. Logging out instantly purges volatile in-memory cache and locks the database, ensuring that rotating colleagues or shared workstation users cannot inspect unauthorized patient files.

❓ How does Murshid's local performance compare to cloud-based EHR platforms?

Cloud-based EHRs rely on network roundtrips and remote database queries that often lag or freeze during network congestion. Murshid delivers sub-5ms query times directly from on-device RAM and local processors, even when filtering complex nutrition datasets.

❓ Does Murshid autonomously make clinical diagnoses or medical decisions?

No. Murshid is an administrative, computational, and documentation assistant, not a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD). It provides no autonomous diagnostic conclusions or prescription engines. All medical decisions and dietary judgments remain the sole purview of the licensed dietitian.
