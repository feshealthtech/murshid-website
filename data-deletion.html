Privacy & Data Management

Data Deletion Request

We respect your right to have full control over your data

This page is dedicated to requesting the deletion of your account data in the Murshid app. You can submit your request via the form below, and we will process it within 30 days.

💡 Faster Alternative: You can delete your data directly from within the app without needing to fill out any form:

Settings ⚙️ ← Account 👤 ← "Delete Account & Data" 🗑️

What gets deleted?

✅ Cloud Account Data
  • Your name and email (Firebase)
  • Gold Points balance (Firestore)
  • Financial transaction history
  • Synced app settings
⏱ Deleted within 30 days of approving your request
⚠️ Local Data on Your Phone
  • Your patients' data (Isar DB)
  • Uploaded logos and images
  • Local settings
We do not own this data and cannot delete it remotely.
Delete the app or clear its data from phone settings: Settings ← Apps ← Murshid ← Clear Data.
ℹ️ Google Drive Backup
  • If you previously enabled backup
  • You will find your files in your Google Drive account
  • Folder: Apps ← Murshid
You can manually delete it from drive.google.com ← Storage ← Backups
⚖️ Legal Note Financial transaction records (invoices and purchases) may be retained for up to 90 days from the date of the deletion request for accounting and financial compliance purposes stipulated in relevant regulations, after which they are permanently and completely deleted and cannot be recovered.

Deletion Request Submitted Successfully!

Thank you for confirming your request. We have successfully received your account data deletion request and it is being forwarded for review and completion of deletion from all our cloud servers within 30 days. We will keep you updated on the progress of your request via email.

Request Processing Timeline

1
Day 1
Request Receipt and Immediate Confirmation
Once your request is received, an immediate confirmation email is sent to you containing a reference number for your request to track it later.
2
Days 1 – 7
Identity and Eligibility Verification
Our team verifies that the requester is the actual account owner, and we may contact you for confirmation if we need additional information.
3
Days 7 – 30
Gradual Data Deletion from All Systems
Your cloud account data is gradually deleted from Firebase, Firestore, and all our internal systems. Transaction records are retained until the legal retention period expires.
4
Day 30
Deletion Completion Confirmation
We will send you a final email confirming the completion of the deletion of all cloud data, detailing what has been deleted and what will be deleted later (billing records).
