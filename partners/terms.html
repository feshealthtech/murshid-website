1 Preamble, Definitions, Contracting Party & Scope

This document (“Charter”) constitutes the governing regulatory framework for the partnership between «Murshid Platform Management»—the owner, developer, and operator of the «Murshid» platform and applications (Murshid Platform — FES HealthTech Ecosystem), represented in contracting by the legal owner and developer or the authorized entity whose name and official capacity are specified in the signed Execution Contract with the Partner—and the Partner (whether a licensed manufacturer, authorized agent, or official distributor registered with the competent national regulatory authorities).

Approved Definitions:

  • The Platform / Ecosystem: The «Murshid» dietetic practice and nutritional calculation software, the «Murshid Companion» client app, and the Murshid Knowledge Center.
  • The Two Dietary Stations (Non-SaMD Dietary Accounting): (1) Prior Dietary Assessment: calculating habitual daily nutrient-intake gaps against standard Recommended Dietary Allowance / Dietary Reference Intake values (RDA / DRI); and (2) Current Meal Plan Construction: calculating the remaining gaps in the designed meal plan and completing them.
  • Compound Slot: A fixed-term (6 or 12 months) preferential right granted to one Partner per nutritional compound per country, covering the four channels defined in Article 3, without creating any monopoly over the general directory or practitioner choice.
  • Verified Suggested Nutritional Option: The Partner's product card, shown with a transparent badge [Suggested Option • Verified Partner] when a compound gap is detected in either station, enabling one-click inclusion while free search of the general directory remains available.
  • Open General Directory: The Platform's database of licensed supplements, open to listing by all licensed companies under Article 3.
  • Murshid Knowledge Center: The Platform's practical educational section, defined in Article 4.
  • Daily Nutritional Knowledge Cards: Concise cards on the practitioner's main dashboard carrying a clear sponsorship badge.
  • Execution Contract: The signed contract and attached payment schedule, which is the sole binding contractual and financial instrument between the parties.

2 Strict 2-Station Dietary Accounting & Non-SaMD Disclosure

🔒 Founding Principle One: Dietary Accounting Only—No Medical Claims

The supplement suggestion mechanism in Murshid is confined exclusively and entirely to the two dietary stations defined in Article 1: «Prior Dietary Assessment» and «Current Meal Plan Construction». The supplement process never operates on laboratory results, medical diagnoses, diseases, or symptoms; it does not read or process any lab tests or clinical data, and no supplement is ever linked to any health condition or treatment.

Non-SaMD Regulatory Notice:

  • Murshid is a digital practice-management, nutritional calculation, and meal-planning software utility. It is not Software as a Medical Device (Non-SaMD) and provides no medical diagnosis or independent treatment prescription.
  • One-click suggestion mechanism: When a gap in daily intake versus standard reference values (RDA / DRI) is detected in either station, the system displays the dietary gap alert by generic scientific name and daily shortfall, then highlights beneath it the slot holder's «Verified Suggested Nutritional Option» card—while keeping a visible secondary option to search the open general directory freely or enter a generic nutrient name only.
  • Absolute Practitioner Autonomy: The dietitian retains full professional freedom to accept or ignore the suggestion or to select any other licensed product, without any programmatic restriction, delay, or algorithmic coercion.
  • Visual Package Clarity: Once the dietitian approves a supplement, the Printed Nutritional Report and the «Murshid Companion» app display its commercial and scientific name, required daily concentration, and the high-resolution official package image. This exists solely to clarify the dietitian's chosen option for bridging the daily dietary gap and to prevent confusion or wrong-strength purchases, with full respect for the client's right of choice and the licensed pharmacist's role in advising on registered alternatives of the same strength.

3 Competition Law Compliance, Open Competition Cycles & the General Directory Policy

⚖️ Founding Principle Two: Fixed-Term Slots & an Open Directory Without Market Foreclosure

In alignment with applicable competition and antitrust laws in the target market, exclusionary practices are strictly prohibited: the general supplement directory remains open to listing by all licensed companies, and practitioners' freedom of search and selection is preserved at all times.

I — What is reserved to the slot holder versus what stays open to competitors:

Reserved to the Slot Holder During the Contract Term (6 or 12 months) Always Open to All Competing Companies & Practitioners
1. The «Verified Suggested Nutritional Option [Suggested Option • Verified Partner]» one-click card when the compound gap is detected in either dietary station. 1. The open right to request listing of licensed products in the general directory under the procedure below.
2. Sponsorship of the compound's practical educational track in the Knowledge Center, using its supplements as practical examples. 2. Full freedom of search and selection for the dietitian in the general directory, or entry of any supplement or generic name, without restriction.
3. Appearance in the Daily Nutritional Knowledge Card on the practitioner's main dashboard for that compound. 3. Technical equality: any product selected by the dietitian appears in client reports and the «Murshid Companion» app with full technical parity.
4. Coverage of all licensed strengths and dosage forms of the Partner under the same compound. 4. Equal right to compete for the slot when each contract cycle ends.

II — Open competition cycles; no right of first refusal:

  • Slots are granted for fixed contract cycles (6 or 12 months) and are opened to fair, open competition among all eligible companies in the market when each cycle ends.
  • There is no exclusive renewal priority, right of first refusal, or permanent seat retention for any Partner, and prior contracting creates no vested right to a subsequent cycle.
  • The slot price in each cycle is determined by fair market competition, supply and demand, and platform growth.

III — General directory listing policy and the nominal administrative fee:

  • The Platform does not automatically collect or list all products on the market; it opens directory listing to all licensed companies and agents, and each company wishing to list its products bears responsibility for contacting the Platform directly and submitting official licenses and product composition.
  • Listing requests are subject to a nominal, unified administrative and technical fee covering request processing, verification of licenses and regulatory documents, and technical indexing of the Supplement Facts panel.
  • This fee is non-refundable where the review reveals missing, defective, or expired licenses or non-conformity with regulatory standards, as it compensates the actual administrative review service. Slot holders and Founding Partners are exempt from cataloging fees for products covered by their slots.

IV — Zero disparagement: Educational tracks, daily cards, or any Platform channel may not be used to disparage or defame any competing product.

4 Murshid Knowledge Center, Practical Tracks & Certificates of Attendance and Completion

The app includes the «Murshid Knowledge & Educational Tracks Center» offering courses on professional use of the system and specialized courses in nutrition science and diet design:

  • Track sponsorship: The slot holder sponsors a specialized educational track tied to its compound, clearly badged [Educational Track Sponsored by: Partner Brand].
  • Practical nutritional examples: The Partner's supplements are explained inside the course as practical nutritional examples: reading the Supplement Facts panel, molecular form and bioavailability, and how dietitians practically select them inside Murshid to bridge daily RDA gaps in the two dietary stations—without any link to lab tests, diagnoses, or symptoms.
  • Certificates of Attendance & Completion: Upon completion, the practitioner receives a digital «Certificate of Attendance & Completion of a Knowledge Track» issued by the Murshid Knowledge Center and referencing the Partner's sponsorship.
  • Regulatory qualification: Issued certificates are «attendance and completion statements for enrichment knowledge and practical content within the Platform» and are not academic certificates or accredited Continuing Medical Education (CME) hours for official licensing or professional classification by any government authority.
  • Daily Nutritional Knowledge Cards: Displayed on the main dashboard with a clear sponsorship badge, highlighting nutritional and molecular properties without any therapeutic or disease-related claims.
  • Editorial independence: The Platform's content team prepares and reviews educational content; the sponsor may only supply official specifications for its product.

5 Unrestricted Professional & Academic Grants for «Murshid Pro» Subscriptions

🎁 Founding Principle Three: Unconditional Support Under Five Strict Safeguards

The Platform enables the Partner to fund batches of annual activation vouchers (Annual Pro Subscription Grant Vouchers) offered as free digital and professional empowerment grants to dietitians, emerging clinics, and final-year nutrition students and graduates, under the Partner's brand sponsorship, subject to the following five mandatory safeguards:

  • Safeguard 1 — Absolute unconditionality: The grant is unrestricted professional and educational support enabling practitioners and students to access modern nutritional calculation tools; accepting it creates no contractual, professional, or ethical obligation to choose, prefer, or recommend any of the granting company's products.
  • Safeguard 2 — Algorithmic neutrality & irrevocable term: The granted account operates with the same full neutrality as any paid account (free directory search, any competing product, generic names), and the grant remains valid for its annual term; the granting company may not request its withdrawal or suspension based on the practitioner's nutritional selections.
  • Safeguard 3 — Non-personal professional tool: The grant is limited to professional nutrition-practice software activation vouchers (Digital Practice Software Vouchers) and cannot be converted to cash or personal benefits.
  • Safeguard 4 — Academic & CSR inclusion: The program includes final-year students and new nutrition graduates alongside practicing dietitians.
  • Safeguard 5 — Legitimate institutional recognition: A non-interfering recognition badge appears on the granted account's dashboard: [Account Activated Under the Unrestricted Professional Empowerment Grant Sponsored by: Partner Brand].

Conversely, any cash commissions, monetary rewards, loyalty points, or personal gifts to practitioners in exchange for selecting or prescribing any product are prohibited, and any side agreement to the contrary is void.

6 Unified Eligibility Criteria & Vetting Protocol (FRAND)

The following four admission criteria are applied objectively and equally to all companies applying for Compound Slots or General Directory listing:

  • 1. Official registration & marketing authorization: Valid registration and marketing authorization from the competent national food & drug authority, ministry of health, or national regulator in the target country.
  • 2. Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP / ISO 22000): Official proof that the manufacturing facility holds current cGMP or ISO 22000 certification.
  • 3. Certificate of Analysis (COA) & Supplement Facts: Submission of an accredited batch COA, the official Supplement Facts panel, and the high-resolution official package image.
  • 4. Bioavailability & standard safety limits (RDA / UL): Peer-reviewed documentation of the compound's molecular form, with recommended daily servings within standard safe dietary thresholds.

Platform Management reserves absolute discretion to accept or reject any sponsorship or listing application without any obligation to state reasons and without liability.

8 99.9% Technical Activation SLA, No Case Guarantees & Forward-Looking Statements

🛡️ Technical & Operational Activation Guarantee (99.9% SLA)

The Platform contractually guarantees continuous technical and programmatic activation at 99.9% of all four channels (Suggested Option in Prior Dietary Assessment, Suggested Option in Current Meal Plan, Daily Knowledge Card, and Knowledge Center course) across 100% of actually active practitioner and clinic accounts within the contracted country throughout the contract term. In the event of any technical downtime or operational delay by the Platform on any channel, the Platform will compensate the Partner with an automatic free extension of the contract term.

  • No guarantee of cases: The Platform makes no promise of predetermined quotas of prescriptions, nutritional-deficiency cases, or selection counts, as these depend on clients' natural dietary patterns and the practitioner's independent professional judgment.
  • Automatic benefit from growth: The Partner automatically benefits, at no additional charge, from every new clinic and practitioner joining the Platform in its country during the contract term.
  • No clinic-count commitments: Neither this Charter nor any contract derived from it includes any numerical commitment to a specific number of clinics, practitioners, or clients.

Forward-Looking Growth Statements:

  • Any figures published on the website or in introductory materials regarding total registered practitioners worldwide (such as «+1,000 Registered Dietitians Globally») or future expansion targets (such as «10,000 Regional Expansion Target») are general ecosystem-level statistics and strategic growth aspirations.
  • Such figures do not constitute a promise or contractual guarantee of any specific number of clinics or practitioners in any particular country.

9 Official Monthly Report, Zero-PII Privacy & Partner App Roadmap

🚨 Absolute Prohibition on Disclosing Personal or Health Data

The Platform adheres to applicable data protection and privacy laws and standards, and absolutely prohibits selling, renting, sharing, or disclosing any personal or health data of clients, as well as disclosing the names of dietitians or clinics that did or did not select a Partner's product, in order to protect practitioner independence and client privacy.

I — Official Monthly Aggregated Performance Report: On the 10th day of each calendar month, the Platform issues and sends the Partner an official aggregated report for the preceding month, strictly limited to 4 anonymized aggregate metrics:

  • 1. Suggested Option Card impressions: The number of times the Partner's product card was highlighted in the two dietary stations nationwide.
  • 2. Dietitian selection / inclusion rate: The number and percentage of times practitioners added the Partner's product to nutritional plans.
  • 3. Export count: The total number of nutritional plans and reports exported (printed or to the «Murshid Companion» app) featuring the Partner's product name and package image, counted at the moment of export only, with no tracking of client behavior afterward, adherence to any doses, or reminders.
  • 4. Knowledge engagement indicators: Total Daily Knowledge Card views and total Knowledge Center course attendance/completions and certificates issued.

II — Operational basis for system counters: Recording these four anonymized aggregate counters (Anonymized System Telemetry) is a mandatory operational component of the Platform's terms of use for practitioners and contains no personal data.

III — «Unified Partner App & Portal» roadmap:

  • As part of its technical roadmap, the Platform is building a unified agent app and portal (partner login, license upload, product listing requests, slot competition, and report viewing), which will be made available to approved Partners upon official launch.
  • Reference to this app falls within the future Product Roadmap and its launch timing creates no contractual obligation or breach, provided the Platform delivers the official monthly report on the 10th of each month.

10 2027 Founding Partner Privileges & Financial Terms

Companies joining the 2027 inaugural cohort receive the following contractual privileges, none of which closes competition or removes other companies' right to apply:

  • +30 days free onboarding: A complimentary month for license verification, product setup, and educational-track preparation before the contract term begins counting.
  • 25% preferential discount for 5 years off the competitive market price: The slot price in each cycle is set by supply, demand, and competition; the Founding Partner enjoys a fixed 25% discount off the approved market price whenever it contracts for or wins a slot in any cycle during the five years (2027–2031).
  • Priority notification & cataloging fee exemption: Early notice when applications open for new cycles, plus full exemption from the nominal administrative fees for reviewing and indexing its products (with no exclusive renewal priority).
  • All strengths and forms, plus Knowledge Center track production: Inclusion and updating of all licensed dosage forms and strengths of the same compound under a single slot at no extra charge.

Financial provisions:

  • Contract as the financial instrument: The signed Execution Contract with its attached payment schedule (and the contractual payment receipt) is the approved financial and contractual document between the parties; the Platform has no obligation to issue tax or commercial invoices unless the contract expressly provides otherwise.
  • Net payments: The Partner shall pay all contractually specified amounts in full, net of any deduction, and alone bears any taxes, withholdings, or bank transfer charges in its jurisdiction.

11 Liability & Indemnification, Regulatory Recall & Suspension, Liability Cap, and Dispute Resolution

⚖️ Product Liability & Full Indemnification

The Partner (manufacturer or authorized agent) alone bears 100% of the legal, civil, criminal, and medical liability for the quality, safety, and integrity of its products, the accuracy of their concentrations and ingredients, their storage and distribution conditions, and any side effects, allergies, or interactions resulting from their use. The Partner shall indemnify, defend, and hold fully harmless, personally and legally, «the Murshid Platform, its Management, Owners, Developers, and Representatives», and bear all amounts, judgments, fines, and legal costs, should any claim, lawsuit, or complaint be brought by any client, healthcare practitioner, regulator, or third party in connection with the Partner's product, its data, its licenses, or its intellectual property rights.

Immediate suspension & termination upon regulatory recall:

  • Upon a decision by the competent national regulator to recall the product or suspend or revoke its license, or upon discovery that any COA or regulatory document submitted by the Partner is inaccurate, the Platform may immediately suspend the product's appearance across all slot channels without prior notice to protect practitioners and clients.
  • The Partner is granted a 15-day window to formally cure the regulatory status or substitute another licensed, conforming product of the same compound; failing that, the Platform may terminate the slot contract permanently and re-offer the slot, and the breaching Partner may not claim a refund of any fees paid for the remaining term, since termination results from its breach of license and eligibility conditions.

Limitation of liability cap:

  • The Platform, its Management, Owners, and Developers bear no personal or contractual liability under any circumstances for indirect or consequential damages, lost profits, reduced commercial sales of the Partner, or any misuse of the system by a practitioner or client.
  • The maximum aggregate financial liability of the Platform, its Owners, and Developers toward the Partner for any contractual or technical claim shall not exceed the unexpended remaining portion of the fees actually paid by the Partner for the active contract cycle.

Dispute resolution & governing law:

  • Any disputes shall first be settled amicably within 60 days of either party's notice of the dispute.
  • Failing that, the dispute is subject to the governing law, jurisdiction, or arbitration (including online arbitration) expressly specified in the signed Execution Contract between the parties.
