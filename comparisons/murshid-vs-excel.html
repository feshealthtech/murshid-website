Frequently Asked Questions: Migrating from Excel

💡 Do I need to re-enter all my food data when switching to Murshid?

No. Murshid comes pre-loaded with an extensive 13,000-item verified food database, proven metabolic calculation formulas, and 70+ clinical protocol starter templates. You won't spend days building spreadsheet formulas; you can start designing balanced meal plans immediately.

⏱️ How much time does designing a meal plan take in Murshid vs. Excel?

In Excel, researching foods, calculating portions, and formatting cells takes 45 to 60 minutes per patient. In Murshid, thanks to single-tap meal replication, automated macro distribution dials, and instant exchange point suggestions, designing a comprehensive plan takes just 5 to 10 minutes.

🔒 Will my patient records remain securely on my personal hardware?

Yes, 100%. Murshid is built on a strict offline-first architecture. All patient profiles, biometric charts, and financial transactions are stored and encrypted locally on your device via the high-speed Isar database. No clinical records are uploaded or exposed to third-party cloud servers.

📱 Can I run Murshid on tablets and mobile devices?

Yes. Murshid is engineered for modern mobile and tablet workflows (Android and iOS). You can conduct consultations, track patient measurements, and print PDF meal plans directly from your tablet or phone without being tied to a bulky laptop.

⚖️ Does Murshid provide automated medical diagnoses?

No. Murshid is strictly an administrative and computational productivity tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses or autonomous therapeutic prescriptions. The qualified licensed dietitian maintains full clinical responsibility for all nutritional plans and patient evaluations.
